# Brag Plan: SAT-SA (Supervisory Analytics Tool for SOC Assessment)

## What is this app?
An air-gapped, sovereign cybersecurity intelligence platform for NCIIPC (National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre) to audit, score, and detect operational blindness and negligence across India's Critical Sector Entities (CSEs).

## The angle
In national critical infrastructure, the danger isn't just cyberattacks — it's SOC operational blindness. When analysts close active 85k-SYN SSH brute force breaches in 4 minutes as "IT guy forgot password", SAT-SA uncovers the negative space with 64-bit SimHash log deduplication and an air-gapped AI Threat Copilot.
